Exemplary signs up as Client-Partner in Hawthorne Consultancy's MOD platform

MOD Contract Signing

View the PR Release-IMAGE

Arleen Ayala engaged Hawthorne Consultancy to aggregate, brand and promote her company's cleaning services under the MOD platform last April 23, 2026. Hawthorne Consultancy's Lead Consultant, Eloisa Corpuz, has since developed the Maid-on-Demand brand of cleaning service with its main platform housed in the www.modworkforce.com website. Arleen's Exemplary Cleaning Services will pilot this program and be the pioneer service provider in this platform, making every home clean and cozy for the modern Filipino family.

The service is the first of its kind in the Philippines which offers trustworthy and professionalized cleaning services that guarantee high-quality and efficient service with just one click or call. This cleaning service's inception was brought on by the rising salary of a stay-in maid and the peripheral costs of keeping one, like the maid's consumption of food and electricity as well as the provision of other mandated government contributions. It also addresses the varying concerns on safety when you let in a stranger into the house. With Maid-on-Demand, these cost and security concerns are addressed while still allowing the modern Filipino family to pursue their individual career, school and personal goals unimpeded by the mundane tasks of housekeeping. Maid-on-Demand allows the modern Filipino family to reserve their time, energy and focus on achieving their goals and enjoying their lives without incurring too much expense or having to let in a stranger into their home.

Maid-on-Demand is the answer to optimal living in the MODern world. Contact us today or visit the platform at www.modworkforce.com to start living your life to the fullest.
